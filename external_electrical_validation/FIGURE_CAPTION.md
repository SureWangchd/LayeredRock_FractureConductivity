# Figure caption

**Electrical measurements and finite-element predictions for artificial sandstones at 5 MPa.** (a) Measured conductivities from Han et al. (2022) and predictions using two background-porosity conventions: pore volume normalized by the specimen volume (Bulk) or by the remaining matrix volume (Matrix). I-0 and I-90 calibrate the backgrounds; I-45 and all three fractured specimens are excluded from calibration. Measurement bars show the reported ±2% tolerance. (b) Predicted fracture increments (filled symbols) and measured conductivities minus transferred backgrounds (open symbols). Bars propagate the reported conductivity and porosity tolerances through the background calibration; they are deterministic ranges, not confidence intervals. These differences are inferred across different specimens. (c) Aperture scenarios at fixed measured total porosity, using the Bulk convention. Reducing crack volume reallocates pore volume to the background; zero aperture is not removal at fixed matrix properties. (d) Changes in total conductivity relative to the finest of four meshes. Panels (a,b) use the finest mesh; sensitivity and tolerance propagation use the second and third meshes, respectively. The observations were obtained at 1 kHz; simulations use the conductive approximation described in the methods.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggab386. Background-transfer procedure: https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggab387.

Input rows: six specimens at a preselected pressure, including two calibration specimens; three fractured targets and one intact holdout are displayed. Other pressure states remain archived. No statistical hypothesis test is performed.
