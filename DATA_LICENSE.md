The original sandstone images are from the Imperial College Consortium on Pore-scale Imaging and Modelling (2014), S1 and S5 datasets, https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.1189274.v1 and https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.1189281.v1. Their original records and attribution apply to the image data. Dong and Blunt (2009), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.80.036307, describes the associated pore-image study.
